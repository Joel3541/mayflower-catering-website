ABOUT MAYFLOWER

Passion for Food.
Commitment to Excellence.

Mayflower Catering Services is based on Rev Dagadu Street in Community 10, Tema, serving clients throughout Tema, Accra and the wider Greater Accra Region.

We combine quality ingredients, creativity and professional service to deliver unforgettable experiences for you and your guests — from a wedding for four hundred to a private dinner for eight.

Alongside catering, our kitchen serves dine-in, takeaway and delivery — from banku with tilapia and our breakfast hampers to the meat pies and pastries our regulars keep coming back for.
